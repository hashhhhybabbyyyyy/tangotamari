Our Story

Where Nikkei
Comes Home

Nikkei is the story of Japanese immigrants who settled in Peru over a century ago, blending their culinary traditions with the bold, vivid flavours of Latin America to create one of the world's most celebrated fusion cuisines.

Tango Tamari brings that tradition to Juhu — crafted by Head Chef Mitesh Rangras and founded by Sarabjit and Rishan Keer, the minds behind iconic Mumbai institutions including Alfredo's, The Butler, and Bayleaf.
